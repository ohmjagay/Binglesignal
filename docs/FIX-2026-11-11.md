# BingleSignal fix guide: READ/WATCH "server error" + stale briefing (11 Nov 2026)

**Result you get:** Gmail sign-in opens READ and WATCH, and fresh stories with DeepSeek summaries arrive automatically every 6 hours.
**Time needed:** about 25 minutes. Do the steps **in order**.

---

## What was wrong (plain English)

1. **The "server error" is a missing-settings problem, not a bug in your pages.** Your live Cloudflare Worker (the name in your log is **`binglesignalv1`**) cannot see its Supabase settings.
   - `/api/teaser` crashed in 17 ms, at the exact line that calls Supabase (far too fast for a real network call).
   - `/api/pool` (READ and WATCH) stopped at the exact line that throws "SUPABASE_URL ... must be configured".
   - The website itself is static, so it still loads. That is why the site looks half alive.
   - Usual causes: the settings were added in the wrong place (the *Build* section), added as type **Text** (a deploy wipes those), or added to a different Worker.
2. **Stories are 5 days old because the refresh was switched off.** Commit `6686fc4` on 10 Nov ("pause briefing automation") removed the 6-hourly schedule. Nothing has refreshed since 7 Nov.
3. **A second bug was hiding behind the first.** Even with the settings fixed, READ would still have failed. One page load downloaded about **82 MB** from Supabase and sent about **24 MB** to the browser, because every story carried its 384-number AI "meaning vector" up to three times. A Cloudflare Worker gets 128 MB of memory, and Supabase's free plan allows only 5 GB of transfer a month, so about 60 page loads would have used it up. Now it is about 3.6 MB from Supabase and 2.7 MB to the browser, and the answer is cached for 30 minutes.
4. **Your account's free trial has most likely ended.** Your database gave early sign-ups a fixed trial end date of 8 Nov (newer sign-ups get 7 days). After Step 1 you may see "An active Lite or Pro plan is required". Step 3a fixes that for your account.
