About BingleSignal

BingleSignal turns the daily flood of news into a 15-minute briefing for people who have decisions to make.

Why it exists

Busy professionals do not have two hours to scroll headlines, and most news feeds are built to keep you scrolling, not to let you finish. BingleSignal is built the other way round: a finite briefing, shaped by the interests and region you choose, that tells you when you are done.

What you get

  • A short daily briefing of the stories that matter for your interests and region, rebuilt every 6 hours.
  • READ: a clear summary of every story with a “why it matters” line, ranked for you.
  • WATCH: video picks with a short note on what each one covers.
  • Every story links to its original source. We summarise and point; we do not copy the publisher’s article.

See exactly how stories are chosen on How we choose news, and what is free and what is paid on the pricing page.

Who is behind it
