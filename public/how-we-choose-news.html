How we choose the news

No human editor picks your stories and no ad budget buys a place on the page. Here is exactly what happens, in order.

  1. We collect from many sources. Every few hours a program reads headlines from newspapers, broadcasters, wire services and official sources (such as regulators), Google News topic feeds for India, the US, the UK and Germany, Medium and Hacker News for what the tech and startup community is reading, and YouTube for videos.
  2. We keep what a business reader needs. Shopping deals, coupons, gift guides and sponsored posts are removed. Light stories (zoo animals, horoscopes, viral clips) are kept only for readers who chose a lifestyle or entertainment interest. Content-farm websites are blocked.
  3. Big, known outlets come first. Wire services and official sources rank highest, then major newspapers and broadcasters, then respected specialist outlets. A story from a website we do not recognise cannot lead the page, and it appears in the free top stories only when at least two independent outlets report it.
  4. We group repeats. Reports of the same event are combined into one card that shows how many sources cover it, so you read it once.
  5. We check the country. A story is marked India only when it is about India. A story about another country, or a global story, is not marked India just because an Indian outlet or the Indian edition of a news service carried it.
  6. We score every story. The score combines how much we trust the publisher, how fresh the story is (older stories fade), how many independent sources cover it and, for community posts and videos, how people responded.
  7. AI writes the short summaries. For the top stories, AI reads the article text (or the excerpt when the article cannot be read) and writes a short summary and a one-line “why it matters”. These are not edited by a person before you see them, and AI can make mistakes. Always use the link to read the original.
  8. We rank your personal list. In READ and WATCH, the order follows your interests, your region and what you like, save, hide and watch. You can mute a source or tell us “less like this” at any time.
What we do not do. We do not rewrite or fact-check the publishers’ reporting, we do not sell placement, and we do not show third-party ads. Every item links to its original source.
