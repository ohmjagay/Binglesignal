Refund & Cancellation

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from your Account page or by emailing our contact email (shown here once it is published) from your account address (we cancel within 24 hours). You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for, and you are not charged again.

Free trial

The 7-day trial is free and needs no card. Nothing is charged when it ends.

Refunds

  • If you are charged and are not satisfied, email us within 7 days of your first payment for a full refund.
  • Later renewals are not refundable, except for duplicate or mistaken charges, or where the law requires it.
  • Approved refunds go back to the original payment method and usually take 5 to 10 working days, depending on your bank or card.

Failed payments

If a renewal fails we keep your access for 3 days while the payment is retried. After that your plan ends until a payment succeeds.

Recurring payments in India

UPI AutoPay and card mandates follow the RBI e-mandate rules: you approve the mandate once, you are notified before each debit, and you can pause or revoke it in your UPI app or bank at any time.
