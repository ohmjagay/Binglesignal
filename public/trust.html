Trust & data handling

BingleSignal is built to help you understand your selected email and news briefings without selling your data or showing third-party ads.

Email summarizer: what happens

Email summaries read the messages you select, send their text to the configured AI provider to generate a summary, and store only the encrypted summary. Raw message text is not stored. Email is read-only: BingleSignal does not send, delete, move, label, or unsubscribe from your messages.

You can disconnect a mailbox or delete email data from Account → Privacy. You can also revoke access in your Google or Microsoft account.

Sub-processors

  • Cloudflare: website and Worker hosting, security, and inbound email processing when forwarding is configured.
  • Supabase: account sign-in and database storage.
  • Google and Microsoft: sign-in and mailbox access only when you choose those features.
  • Configured AI provider: processes selected email text only to produce the summary you request.
  • Resend or Amazon SES: sends briefings only after delivery is configured and enabled.
  • Telegram: receives a briefing only when you link a Telegram account.
  • Razorpay or the configured international payment provider: processes payments and retains required billing records.
  • GitHub: runs public-news feed updates; it does not receive mailbox data.
