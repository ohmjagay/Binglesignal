Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

BingleSignal ("we", "us") runs binglesignal.com, a personalised news and video briefing. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions or requests: support@binglesignal.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and how you signed in (Google, or email and password).
  • Your preferences: the geographies and interests you choose.
  • Your activity in the app: what you like, save, hide or open, and how long you watch a video. We use this to learn what is useful to you.
  • Technical data: IP address and device information processed by our hosting provider for security and delivery, and small files in your browser storage that keep you signed in and remember your settings.

How we use it

To sign you in, build and rank your personal READ and WATCH feeds, keep the service secure and improve our ranking. We do not sell your personal data, we do not show third-party advertising, and we do not use advertising trackers.

Email Summariser beta

The email beta is currently paused: no mailboxes are being read or summarised. If you connected Gmail or Outlook, the beta uses read-only OAuth permissions that allow access to email content. When you request a summary and separately consent, selected inbox message text, subject, sender and received time are sent to the AI summarisation endpoint configured for the beta. The endpoint processes this text to generate your report. Do not request a summary unless you are comfortable with this processing. BingleSignal does not store raw message bodies, but stores generated summaries and job status for up to 30 days. Disconnecting the mailbox deletes its associated summary jobs and generated summaries; you can also revoke access in your Google or Microsoft account. Metadata sync and scheduled fixture reports do not contain email content and are not AI summaries.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase - sign-in and database (your account, preferences, activity, encrypted mailbox tokens, and generated email summaries).
  • Cloudflare - hosting, delivery, the Turnstile robot check on sign-up and email-beta processing.
  • Google - "Continue with Google" sign-in and Gmail mailbox access when you connect Gmail.
  • Microsoft - Outlook mailbox access when you connect a Microsoft account.
  • DeepSeek (a China-based AI company) - writes the short summaries of public news articles and the notes on videos. It receives only public article and video text (headlines, excerpts, public article text, descriptions), never your account details, preferences or activity. The summaries are written by AI and are not edited by a person before you see them; see How we choose news. If the email beta is switched back on, the email text you select for a summary is also sent to DeepSeek.
  • PayPal - processes subscription payments made with PayPal. BingleSignal never sees your PayPal login or card details.
  • Razorpay - processes subscription payments made by card, UPI or netbanking. BingleSignal never sees your card details.
  • YouTube - videos play in YouTube's embedded player (privacy-enhanced mode). Watching a video is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Yahoo Finance - our server fetches delayed index levels (such as Sensex, Nifty and Nasdaq) for the Markets panel. Your browser never contacts Yahoo and no personal data is sent.
  • DuckDuckGo - our server fetches the small logos of news websites from DuckDuckGo's public icon service and keeps a copy. Your browser only talks to BingleSignal and no personal data is sent.
  • GitHub - builds our feeds automatically. It never receives your personal data.

Third-party content

Headlines, summaries, posts and videos come from publishers, communities and creators. Every item links to its original source, whose own policies apply once you leave our site.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. You can ask us to delete your data at any time, and you can clear the data stored in your browser through your browser settings.

Google API data

BingleSignal's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal data and to withdraw consent, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the EU and UK GDPR and the California CCPA where they apply to you. Contact us using the email above.

Children

BingleSignal is not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Security and changes

We use encrypted connections and access controls, but no system is perfectly secure. If we change this policy we will update the date above.
