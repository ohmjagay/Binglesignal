Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

BingleSignal ("we", "us") runs binglesignal.com, a personalised news and video briefing. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions or requests: our contact email (to be published shortly).

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and how you signed in (Google, or email and password).
  • Your preferences: the geographies and interests you choose.
  • Your activity in the app: what you like, save, hide or open, and how long you watch a video. We use this to learn what is useful to you.
  • Technical data: IP address and device information processed by our hosting provider for security and delivery, and small files in your browser storage that keep you signed in and remember your settings.

How we use it

To sign you in, build and rank your personal READ and WATCH feeds, keep the service secure and improve our ranking. We do not sell your personal data and we do not show third-party advertising.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase - sign-in and database (your account, preferences and activity).
  • Cloudflare - hosting and delivery.
  • Google - the "Continue with Google" sign-in.
  • YouTube - videos play in YouTube's embedded player (privacy-enhanced mode). Watching a video is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Wikimedia - background paragraphs on stories are loaded from Wikipedia in your browser.
  • GitHub - builds our feeds automatically. It never receives your personal data.

Third-party content

Headlines, summaries, posts and videos come from publishers, communities and creators. Every item links to its original source, whose own policies apply once you leave our site.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. You can ask us to delete your data at any time, and you can clear the data stored in your browser through your browser settings.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal data and to withdraw consent, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the EU and UK GDPR and the California CCPA where they apply to you. Contact us using the email above.

Children

BingleSignal is not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Security and changes

We use encrypted connections and access controls, but no system is perfectly secure. If we change this policy we will update the date above.
